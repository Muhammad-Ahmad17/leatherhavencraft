## Why NOT Lemon Squeezy

**Payoneer is not a payout option.** Full stop.

Lemon Squeezy's only payout methods are:
- **Bank wire** (via Stripe) → 1% fee per payout for non-US
- **PayPal** → 3% capped at $30 per payout for non-US

Your Citibank/Payoneer account **cannot** be officially selected as a payout destination. You'd be forced to either:
- Use it as a "US bank" for the wire (unofficial, risky — Stripe could flag it)
- Use PayPal (extra 3% fee, and PayPal merchant accounts are unreliable in Pakistan)

Plus: Lemon Squeezy is **transitioning to Stripe Managed Payments** (announced Jan 2026), which adds more Stripe dependency — and Stripe doesn't work in Pakistan.

## Why Paddle

| | Paddle | Lemon Squeezy |
|---|---|---|
| **Payoneer payout** | ✅ Official, one-click link | ❌ Not supported |
| Payout fee from platform | **$0** (only Payoneer's own fees apply) | 1% (bank) or 3% (PayPal) |
| Stripe dependency | None | Increasing (2026 transition) |
| Pakistan onboarding | Individual, no business docs needed (SaaS) | Lighter but Stripe verification risk |
| Payout schedule | Monthly, $100 min | Twice monthly, $50 min, 13-day hold |

## The exact process with your Payoneer account

Here's what actually happens step by step:

```
1. Customer buys from your store (USD/EUR/GBP)
       ↓
2. Paddle processes payment (5% + $0.50)
       ↓
3. Paddle accumulates balance in your Paddle dashboard
       ↓
4. On the 1st of each month (if balance ≥ $100):
   Paddle sends payout → directly to your Payoneer account
       ↓
5. Money lands in your Payoneer balance (USD)
   (This is the same account that gives you the Citibank
   receiving details you shared — you don't enter those
   anywhere. Paddle links to your Payoneer account directly.)
       ↓
6. You withdraw from Payoneer → Pakistani bank
   (HBL real-time withdrawal available since April 2026)
```

**Key point:** You never enter your Citibank account number, routing number, or SWIFT code anywhere. In Paddle's payout settings, you just **link your Payoneer account** (log in with your Payoneer credentials). Paddle sends the money to Payoneer's system, and it appears in your balance. The Citibank details are just a *receiving alias* — useful if someone else wants to wire you USD directly, but irrelevant for Paddle→Payoneer flow.

### Cost per $100 sale (international customer)

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| Paddle fee | 5% + $0.50 = $5.50 |
| FX margin (if customer paid in non-USD) | ~2–3% (baked in) |
| Paddle → Payoneer payout | **$0** |
| Payoneer → PKR bank withdrawal | ~$1–2 (or free via HBL) |
| **You keep** | **~$93–94** |

With Lemon Squeezy, that same $100 would cost you an extra 1% (bank) or 3% (PayPal) on payout → you'd keep **~$91–92** at best, with more friction.
